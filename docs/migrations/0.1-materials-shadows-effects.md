# Migrating to surfaces, shadows and screen effects

Decisions [019](../adr-019-surface-materials-and-reflections.md),
[020](../adr-020-sun-shadows.md) and [021](../adr-021-ambient-occlusion-and-bloom.md)
add material surfaces, sun shadows, ambient occlusion and bloom.

- Scenes look different by default: the sun casts shadows, creases darken and
  bright light glows. Remove an effect's work with
  `world.resource_mut::<Shadows>().enabled = false`, or an `AmbientOcclusion`
  or `Bloom` intensity of 0. Set `Shadows::bounds` to the scene's region for
  the sharpest, stable shadows; without it the maps follow the camera.
- Sun highlights are physically based: matte surfaces shine less than before
  and a faint sky sheen appears at grazing angles. Give materials a surface
  with `Material::new(color)?.with_surface(Surface { roughness: 0.2, ..Surface::MATTE })?`.
  `Atmosphere::specular` now scales highlights and reflections (1 is
  physical, as in every preset), and the new `Atmosphere::emission` scales
  material emission; struct literals of `Atmosphere` add it.
- `RenderInstance` has a `surface` field, and `RenderSnapshot` has
  `elapsed_seconds`, `shadows`, `ambient_occlusion` and `bloom`. Code building
  them with struct literals adds the fields or uses `..Default::default()`;
  engine extraction fills them.
- Texture alpha masks emission. `Texture::new` still requires opaque alpha;
  use `Texture::with_emission_mask` for masks. A material with emission and
  an opaque texture, or none, emits over its whole surface.
- Scene version 5 authors an optional material `surface`. Importing now
  writes version 5; authored and imported textures stay opaque.
- `RenderFrame::casters` lists every slot that may cast a shadow, culled or
  visible, with `caster_revision`.
- The effect resources are optional ([decision 022](../adr-022-scene-environments-and-agent-controls.md)):
  removing `Shadows`, `AmbientOcclusion` or `Bloom` turns the effect off, and
  the renderer only creates an effect's pipelines once it runs. Systems that
  read them with `Res<...>` either keep the defaults inserted or use
  `Option<Res<...>>`.
- A version 5 scene may author an `environment`. Instantiating one sets all
  four look resources, overriding values set in code before it; documents
  without one leave them unchanged.
- `RenderStats` has `shadows`, `ambient_occlusion` and `bloom`, and browser
  stats the matching `shadow_*`, `ambient_occlusion` and `bloom` fields.
  `draw_calls` still counts the camera pass only; add `shadow_draws` for
  every submitted draw.
