# Migrating to shadow cascades and receiver planes

[Decision 028](../adr-028-shadow-cascades-and-receiver-planes.md) splits the
near shadow map into cascades and compares lookups against receiver planes.

- Near shadows are sharper and shadows touch their casters; `Shadows`
  literals add `cascades` (3 by default, 1 for one near map) or use
  `..Shadows::default()`.
- Scene version 6 authors `environment.shadows.cascades`; versions 1 to 5
  refuse it with `version_required`.
- Custom shaders that bind the first group of the main passes: binding 3 is
  a `texture_depth_2d_array` of the cascades, binding 7 a non-filtering
  sampler for receiver-plane gathers, and the `Shadow` uniform is 400 bytes
  (per-cascade matrices, reaches, normal offsets and slope limits).
- Shadow maps store depths with one unit of constant bias and no
  slope-scaled bias; comparisons that do not use receiver planes need their
  own margin.
